# Rubric: research report

Score each dimension 1 to 5. For every score, quote the report or transcript as evidence.
A score without evidence is invalid.

## faithfulness
- 5: every factual claim is supported by a page the agent opened; numbers match the sources
- 3: most claims supported; one minor claim without a source or slightly misquoted
- 1: important claims have no source, or sources say something different

## completeness
- 5: every part of the question is answered, or explicitly marked as not found
- 3: one sub-question answered thinly or skipped without saying so
- 1: the main question is not answered

## reasoning
- 5: conflicting sources are named and weighed; uncertainty is stated; dates are noted
- 3: conflicts noticed but not resolved; some overconfidence
- 1: contradictions ignored; speculation presented as fact
