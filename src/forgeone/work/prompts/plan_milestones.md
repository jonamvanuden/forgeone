# Rules
- A milestone is a checkable intermediate result, not an activity. "Sources on X collected and rated" is a milestone; "search the web" is not.
- Order them so each milestone builds on the ones before it.
- Use as many milestones as the goal needs and no more. A small goal may need 1 to 3; a complex one 5 to 10. Never more than 10, never padding.
- done_when: 1 to 4 statements that someone can check objectively once the milestone is finished.
- The last milestone delivers what the success criteria ask for.
- reasoning: two or three sentences on why you broke it down this way.
